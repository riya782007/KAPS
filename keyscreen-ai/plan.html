NEWVORA · Product & Integration Master Plan

Turning Edukey360 into an AI-run recruitment engine

A phased plan to automate the recruiter grunt-work, scale the “100% Verified” promise, and give schools a “hire in days, not weeks” experience — with fewer people and far higher throughput. Prepared for Varun Bahl & Amit ji.

🎓 K–12 · India⚡ AI-first ops🔒 Human-in-the-loop▲ Ships on Vercel

The problem we're removing

Both SOPs show the same bottleneck: manual sourcing & screening. A Junior Recruiter is targeted at 40–50 calls/day; Telecallers read every resume and repeat the same 7-question screen, then hand-tag status and update a tracker. It is pure manpower, it doesn't scale, and it's the slowest step between a school's requirement and a shortlist. KeyScreen AI automates this loop — and the roadmap below extends the same AI leverage across verification, matching, billing, and the planned self-service portal (Model 3).

How it fits together (architecture)

Front-end (Next.js on Vercel — evolves from this POC) → API / serverless (Vercel functions) → AI layer (Claude/GPT for parsing, matching, dialogue) → Database (Supabase/Postgres for candidates, requirements, pipeline) → Integrations (WhatsApp, voice, KYC, payments) → Edukey360 dashboard.

  • Every AI decision is a recommendation, not final — recruiters override in one click (human-in-the-loop).
  • No candidate is contacted without a stored consent flag (aligns with the SOP data-privacy section + India DPDP Act 2023).
  • Verification badges stay owned by the Verification Team; AI surfaces them, never fabricates them.
  • The current POC front-end is already modular — data.js swaps to a live API, app.js logic stays.

Data privacy & safety (non-negotiable)

  • Consent-first outreach — every candidate contact requires a stored consent flag; honour opt-outs immediately.
  • India DPDP Act 2023 alignment — purpose limitation, data minimisation, retention & deletion policy for resumes.
  • Least-privilege access — candidate PII visible only to Verification & Operations, exactly as the SOP states.
  • Human-in-the-loop — no auto-reject or auto-hire without a recruiter's confirmation.
  • Secrets hygiene — all keys in env vars (Vercel dashboard), never in the repo; .env.example ships, .env never does.
