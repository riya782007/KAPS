How the 7 modules connect (the flywheel)

🔁 Clockwise flow: Outreach → Parse → Match → Screen → Verify → Place (Portal) → Learn⬅ Feedback: the Ops Brain feeds placement & retention outcomes back into Match & Outreach

The one big idea: every module reads from and writes to the same shared core. A screening call enriches a candidate's graph node; a verified credential upgrades their trust tier; a placement outcome teaches the Match Engine which teachers actually stay. So the system compounds — more teachers & more placements → a smarter graph → better matches → higher retention → happier schools → more teachers. That flywheel is Edukey360's moat, not any single feature.

Why connecting them wins (vs. 7 separate tools)

  • Compounding data: every call, verification and placement enriches one shared graph — competitors starting later can't catch up to your data.
  • Verify once, use everywhere: the Trust Layer means a teacher verified for one school is instantly trusted for the portal and future roles.
  • Outcome-based matching: the feedback loop optimises for retention, directly improving the KPIs in your Operations SOP.
  • One consent + privacy spine: DPDP-compliant consent is enforced once, centrally — not bolted onto 7 tools.
  • Marketplace flywheel: Models 1 & 2 feed the verified pool that makes Model 3 (the portal) valuable — turning services revenue into product revenue.
