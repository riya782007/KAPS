# 🎤 Tab-by-Tab Demo Script — Edukey360 OS

Say these lines as you click each item in the left sidebar. ~30–45 seconds per tab.
Opening line before you start:
> "Everything you'll see runs on one shared data core — the Candidate Knowledge Graph. Do something in one tab and every other tab updates instantly. Let me walk you through it."

---

## 📊 Dashboard — "the founder's cockpit"
> "This is your morning view. Up top: open requirements, candidates AI-screened, interview-ready, and recruiter-hours saved — live. Below that is the part you'll love: **Next Best Actions**. The system tells *you* where to look — high-urgency roles still open, candidates ready to interview, teachers not yet contacted, unverified profiles, and placed teachers at flight-risk. On the right, every action across the company streams in real time, and there's a predictive flight-risk radar for teachers likely to leave — so you act *before* they quit."

## 📋 Requirements — "your live vacancies"
> "Every school opening lives here — role, board, grade, location, salary band, and an SLA target based on urgency. Each card shows how many candidates matched and how many are already interview-ready. You can add a new requirement in seconds, and click any card to jump straight into matching."

## 📡 Sourcing — "the résumé reader + always-on outreach" *(the wow moment)*
> "Watch this. I'll drop in a real teacher's résumé…" *(click a sample chip or paste a CV → **Parse with AI**)*
> "The AI reads it and pulls out everything — subject, experience, qualification, board, current and expected salary, notice period, skills — and shows me exactly what it extracted." *(the reveal card)*
> "One click, it's in the Knowledge Graph." *(**Add to Knowledge Graph**)*
> "And down here, instead of your telecallers dialing all day, the system re-engages your existing database over WhatsApp automatically."

## 🎯 Match — "AI matching that thinks about retention"
> "For any requirement, the AI ranks your pool — but not just on skills. **Fit** is how well they match the role; **Stay** is how likely they are to actually stick around, based on commute, salary gap, and experience. Every match has a plain-English reason. So you're not just filling a seat — you're placing teachers who last. One click screens the whole shortlist."

## 🤖 KeyScreen — "the AI that makes the calls"
> "This is what replaces 40–50 screening calls a day. Hit screen, and an AI voice agent — Maya — runs the exact same 7-question screening, perfectly, every time, and auto-tags the outcome: interested, interview-ready, call-back. No telecaller, no script fatigue, no missed data. Or auto-screen the entire pool in seconds."

## 🔒 Verification — "your '100% Verified' promise, automated"
> "This is your Trust Layer. Every teacher moves through tiers — self-declared, document-verified, then blockchain-verified via DigiLocker. Verify once, and that trust is reused everywhere — in matching, screening, and the school portal. One button verifies the whole pending backlog."

## 🏫 School Portal — "your Model-3 revenue line"
> "This is what schools would pay a subscription for. Their HR just types in plain English — 'CBSE PGT Physics in Gurgaon under 60k' — and instantly gets a ranked list of your **verified** teachers, ready to screen. Same engine, self-service. That turns your services business into a product business."

## 🧠 Knowledge Graph — "one record, shared everywhere"
> "This is the single source of truth — every teacher as one node. Filter by stage, search by anything, and click any teacher for their full 360° view: profile, best-fit roles, screening result, retention prediction, and a complete timeline of everything that's happened. This shared record is the moat — every action makes it smarter."

## ⚙️ Integrations — "how it goes truly live"
> "And when you're ready to make it real, this is where it connects — to WhatsApp, AI voice, verification providers, and billing, through secure workflows. Your API keys stay locked server-side, never in the app. Today it's on sample data so nothing can break; flip this on and it's running your real hiring."

---

## Close
> "That's the whole loop — source, match, screen, verify, place, and learn — running as one system, so your team stops doing the repetitive work and starts doing the work only humans can. Shall we pilot it on one real vacancy?"
