# 🎤 Monday Demo Script — Edukey360 OS for Varun

> Goal: make Varun *feel* the weight of monotonous, repetitive, error-prone work lift off his company.
> Total time: **6–8 minutes**. Keep Live mode OFF (Integrations tab) so nothing can fail.

## Before the call (2 min)
- Open the live Vercel link in full-screen (press F11).
- On the **Dashboard**, click **↺ Reset demo** so numbers start at zero.
- Have the **plan** and **deep dive** tabs ready (they open in new tabs).
- Silence notifications.

---

## Act 1 — Name the pain (60 sec)
> *"Varun, before I show you anything — let me describe a normal week in your recruitment team."*

Click **▶ Guided demo**. The dark "today" screen appears. Read it with him, slowly:

> *"40 to 50 screening calls per recruiter, every single day. The same seven questions, hundreds of times. Manual trackers, human errors, missed follow-ups. And the worst part — great teachers who ghost, and you only find out weeks later."*

Pause. Then:

> *"This is the work quietly burning out your team and costing you placements. Watch what happens when the system does it instead."*

Click **▶ Show me the other way.**

---

## Act 2 — Let it run (3–4 min)
Don't touch anything — just narrate as it moves. The narration bar guides you.

- **Sourcing:** *"It re-engages a teacher from your existing database over WhatsApp — no dialing."*
- **Match:** *"It ranks candidates not just on fit, but on how likely they are to actually stay — with a reason for every match. No keyword guesswork."*
- **KeyScreen (the wow moment):** *"Now watch — an AI voice call runs the entire screening. The same seven questions, perfectly, every time. It collects the answers and tags the result itself."* (Let the call play fully.)
- **Verification:** *"Instant, blockchain-grade verification — your '100% verified' promise, done in seconds and reusable forever."*
- **Place:** *"The vacancy fills, the invoice auto-raises, and it even predicts this teacher's flight risk."* (Confetti fires — smile.)
- **Clears the pool:** *"And it never stops at one — it just cleared your entire shortlist in seconds."*

---

## Act 3 — The payoff (60 sec)
The finale screen counts up the numbers. Let it land, then:

> *"That was a full week of manual calling — done in about a minute. Your recruiters stop dialing and start closing. Your telecallers do work that actually needs a human. And you — you stop worrying about who dropped the ball on a follow-up."*

Read the closing line with him:

> *"No monotony. No repetitive scripts. No manual trackers. No missed follow-ups. Just your team, doing the work only humans can."*

---

## Then hand him the keyboard (1–2 min)
> *"Click anything you want."*
- Open a **candidate** → show the 360° drawer (one shared record).
- **Knowledge Graph** → *"every module reads and writes the same data — this is the moat."*
- **School Portal** → type *"CBSE PGT Physics Gurgaon under 60k"* → *"your schools could self-serve this, as a subscription."*

## Close
> *"Today this runs on sample data so it can't fail in front of you. The exact same screens plug into live data and real AI the moment we flip it on — I've already mapped every integration. Want me to switch it live for a pilot on one real vacancy?"*

---

## Objection handling
- **"Is the AI accurate / will it misjudge teachers?"** → *"Every AI decision is a recommendation, not a verdict — your recruiter overrides in one click. Human-in-the-loop by design."*
- **"Is candidate data safe?"** → *"Consent is enforced centrally, DPDP-aligned, and PII is need-to-know — exactly like your SOP already says."*
- **"How long to go live?"** → *"Phase 1 — real data + AI — is ~2–3 weeks. The migration and keys are already written."*
- **"What's this cost to run?"** → *"Pay-as-you-go on usage; at pilot scale it's small. I've listed every provider and where costs sit."*
