## Roles & Responsibilities
- **Business Development (BD):** Lead gen, first client conversation, proposals, plan recommendation, commercial negotiation.
- **Client Onboarding:** School KYC, contract execution, dashboard account setup, handover to Operations.
- **Talent Sourcing & Verification:** Sourcing, identity verification, credential authentication, background/reference checks, maintaining the verified-educator pool.
- **Operations / Deployment:** AI + human matching, shortlisting, interview coordination, offer facilitation, deployment tracking, replacement management.
- **Finance & Billing:** Invoicing (Model 1 per placement; Model 2 per cycle), payment follow-up, reconciliation, GST compliance.
- **Account Manager (Retainership):** SPOC for retainer clients; requisitions, SLA adherence, renewal.
- **IT / Tech:** Dashboard uptime, access management; owns Model 3 portal build.
- **Training & Development:** NEP 2020-aligned programmes (New Teacher Orientation, Digital Classroom Mastery, Leadership Development, Subject Specialization).

## Cross-Cutting Verification Protocol (identical across Model 1 & 2)
1. **Identity Verification** — Govt ID (Aadhaar/PAN), contact confirmation. Owner: Verification Team. TAT: Same day.
2. **Credential Authentication** — Degree/cert validation, teaching license checks. TAT: 24–48h.
3. **Background & Reference Check** — Prior employment, 2+ references, police verification where required. TAT: 48–72h.
4. **Profile Activation** — Verified badge applied; candidate visible to Operations. TAT: Same day as completion.

*Educator registration & verification is always free to the candidate.*

## Model 1 — Direct Staffing with Simultaneous Billing (Process)
Lead Intake (BD, Day 0) → Requirement Confirmation (BD/Onboarding, Day 0–1) → Sourcing & Verification (1–3 days) → AI + Human Matching (1 day) → Shortlisting & Interview (1–2 days) → Selection & Offer (1 day) → Deployment (billing trigger) → **Simultaneous Billing** (same day as deployment) → Payment Follow-up → Continuous Engagement (Day 7/30/90) → Replacement if needed (within guarantee window, no extra fee).

## Model 2 — Retainership / Contract Staffing (Process)
Institutional Inquiry → Needs Assessment (2–5 days) → Commercial Proposal (3–5 days) → Contract Execution (5–10 days) → Account Setup (dedicated AM) → Staff Requisition (ad hoc via dashboard/AM) → Sourcing & Verification → Matching/Shortlisting/Deployment → Retainer Billing (fixed cycle) → SLA & Replacement Guarantee → Quarterly Business Review → Renewal (T-90 days).

## Model 3 — Self-Service Teacher Resume Portal (Planned, Pre-Launch)
Target state: subscription portal where a school's HR/admin searches, filters, and shortlists from ~1,00,000 verified resumes. Build-out phases: Platform Build → Resume Database Build-Up → Verification-at-Scale Policy → Subscription & Pricing Design → Data Privacy & Consent → Pilot/Beta → Internal Training → Go-Live.

## Escalation Matrix
- **L1** — Routine delay / minor scheduling: Operations Executive, same working day.
- **L2** — SLA breach, billing dispute: Account Manager / Ops Lead, within 24h.
- **L3** — Repeated SLA breaches, verification failure post-deployment: Operations Head, within 48h.
- **L4** — Legal/contractual dispute, data privacy incident: Founders / Legal Counsel, immediate.

## Reporting KPIs (real-time dashboard)
- **Time-to-Hire** — days from confirmed requirement to deployment (Ops Head, weekly)
- **Retention Rate** — % placed staff active at 90/180/365 days (AM, monthly)
- **Cost-per-Hire** — total cost ÷ placements (Finance, monthly)
- **Fill Rate** — % requisitions fulfilled within SLA (Ops Head, weekly)
- **Active Retainer Contracts** — count & value of live Model 2 contracts (BD Lead, monthly)
- **Model 3 Interest Pipeline** — schools interested in the portal (BD Lead, monthly)

## Data Privacy
Candidate personal data restricted to Verification & Operations on need-to-know basis. School commercial terms restricted to BD, Onboarding, Finance, AM. Educator registration free & voluntary. Data shared with schools limited to what's needed for a specific requirement.

## Recruiter SOP Packages (from Recruiter SOP PDF)
- **KeyHire** — "Get Started With Focused Hiring": 4 hiring cycles/session, dedicated support, sourcing from database/job portals, screening, shortlisting, interview coordination. 12-month validity.
- **KeyGrowth** — "For Schools With High Attrition": 6 hiring cycles/session, priority support, sourcing & screening, shortlisting, interview scheduling. 12-month validity.
- **KeyElite** — "Monthly Hiring Support For Growing Schools": 1 cycle/month (max 12/yr), dedicated partnership, priority pipeline access, continuous sourcing, advisory + workforce planning. 12-month validity.

## Internal Team Structure & KRAs (from Recruiter SOP PDF)
- **Business Head → Business Developers → Senior Recruiters → Junior Recruiters → Telerecruiters (portal callers).**
- **BD KRAs:** lead gen, market research, cold calling, client meetings, service presentation, requirement understanding, lead conversion, follow-ups, CRM updates, proposals, relationship building, revenue, retention, competitor tracking.
- **Senior Recruiter KRAs:** requirement understanding & planning, sourcing & pipeline, screening & quality control, client coordination & interview mgmt, closure & joining mgmt, team management. Daily target: 5–8 quality profiles.
- **Junior Recruiter KRAs:** sourcing, candidate data management, initial screening, engagement, recruitment support. Daily: 40–50 sourcing calls, 15–20 new profiles, 8–10 screenings.
- **Telerecruiter KRAs:** candidate calling & engagement, verification, activation, database management, recruitment support. Status tags: Interested / Not Interested / Call Back / Not Reachable / Interview Ready / Joined Elsewhere.
